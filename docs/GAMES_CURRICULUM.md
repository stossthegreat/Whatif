# The Rivlr Games Curriculum

The product thesis: **Rivlr is an engine that throws strangers into hilarious
situations.** Video is the transport; the games are the product. This document
is the researched, rigorously-filtered curriculum behind the pack.

## Research — what actually works (and why)

**1. Jackbox (Quiplash, Fibbage) — the players ARE the content.**
The best party games ever made don't supply the comedy; they extract it.
Quiplash = open prompt + the room votes. Fibbage = players bluff each other.
Lessons: (a) prompts must leave creative room, (b) every loop must close with
the ROOM judging, (c) deception/bluffing is comedy gold.

**2. Truth or Dare / Spin the Bottle apps — random targeting is suspense.**
The bottle mechanic works because of the 2 seconds where nobody knows WHO.
Intensity tiers (mild → bold) let sessions escalate. "Kiss mode" is the #1
requested feature category — flirty energy drives retention. Lessons:
(a) target one person at random, on camera, and the whole room leans in,
(b) escalate intensity across a session, (c) flirty > explicit.

**3. Houseparty (Heads Up, Trivia) — short rounds, camera-native, deck variety.**
Every game was under a minute; decks/variety sustained repeat play; the best
moments used the face and the room (Heads Up = your face IS the game).
Lessons: (a) 30–60s rounds, (b) use the camera (faces, objects, performances),
(c) variety is a retention feature, not a nice-to-have.

## The design laws (every game must obey)

1. **The players are the content.** The prompt extracts THEM — their face,
   story, opinion, performance. Never trivia about the world.
2. **Somebody is always on the spot.** Targeting (the bottle, the point,
   the hot seat) creates the lean-in moment.
3. **The room always judges.** Every round ends with a verdict — a vote,
   a crown, a split. Status is the addiction.
4. **Camera-native or nothing.** If it would work as a text game, it's not
   good enough. Faces, objects, performances, eye contact.
5. **Escalate.** Sessions run warm → wild → spark. Strangers need a warm-up
   question before they'll do a kiss face.
6. **Short.** No round over ~60 seconds. The next one is always coming.

## The quality gate — every prompt is scored before it ships

| Gate | Question | Kill threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 😂 Laugh | Will at least one person laugh out loud? | Must be yes |
| 📷 Camera | Does it use faces/video/objects? | Strongly preferred |
| 🎯 Spot | Does it put someone on the spot? | Preferred |
| 💜 Spark | Does it create connection/flirt energy? | Required for spark tier |
| 🛡️ Safe | App Store safe, 18+ playful not explicit, no harm | Must pass |
| ♻️ Fresh | Does it play differently with different people? | Must be yes |

## The variables engine (never the same room twice)

- **45+ games × deep prompt pools**, options shuffled per round.
- **No repeats:** never the same game kind or name back-to-back; recently
  seen prompts are avoided across rooms.
- **`{target}` slots:** prompts can name a random person in the room —
  the same prompt lands on a different victim every time.
- **The bottle (`spin` kind):** an animated bottle physically picks the
  target. Pure suspense, straight from the research.
- **Session arc:** round 1 draws from `warm` games, the finale draws from
  `spark` (flirty) games, the middle is chaos.
- **Chaos Cards** strike mid-session; **Golden Rooms**, the **Lucky Seat**
  and the **awards ceremony** wrap every session in variable rewards.

## The curriculum (by tier)

### WARM (round 1 — anyone can answer instantly)
Hot Take · Would You Rather · Same Brain · Emoji Only · Green Flag Red Flag ·
Cursed Combos · Delulu Check

### WILD (the middle — performance & judgement chaos)
Point Party · Superlatives · Red Flag · Confession Cam · Truth Meter ·
Hot Seat · Storytime · Petty Court · Speed Debate · Rap Battle · Scam Call ·
Conspiracy Corner · Oscar Bait · Show & Tell · Caption This · Roast Me ·
Worst Impression · Finish The Sentence · Finish The Lyric · One Word Story ·
Five Second Rule · Act It Out · First To Find · Odd One Out · Sound Effect ·
Voice Swap · Sell It · Survival · Fake Flex · **Face Battle** (ugliest face /
fish face / "I saw my ex" face — the pulling-faces game) · Freeze Face ·
Laugh Lock · NPC Mode

### SPARK (the finale — flirty, the dating seed)
**Spin the Bottle** (the bottle picks who answers: "your actual type?",
"rate your own rizz", "who here would you date? 👀") · **Rizz Battle**
(best/worst pickup lines) · **Kiss Face** ("practice your kiss face 😗 HOLD
IT" — the practice-a-snog game, made hilarious) · **Eye Contact** (10 seconds,
no laughing) · **Compliment Battle** · **Date Pitch** · Two Truths ·
First Impression

Spark is deliberately playful-not-explicit: it's the bridge to the long-term
dating vision, and it stays App Store safe. A gated "spicier" tier can ship
later behind explicit opt-in.
