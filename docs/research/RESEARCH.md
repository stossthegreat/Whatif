## A · The compulsion loop — the mechanics of "one more"

**Headline findings**
- **Dopamine is anticipation, not pleasure.** It fires hardest for *unpredictable*
  rewards and ramps to a **maximum at ~50/50 uncertainty**, flattening to zero once
  a reward is predictable (Schultz; Fiorillo et al.).
- **The re-roll is the addictive unit, not the conversation.** In **1.7M
  random-video sessions, 80% lasted <5s, only 1% >30s** (Microsoft Research). People
  binge the *search*, not the payoff.
- **Near-miss effect:** an interaction cut short *just* as it got good recruits
  win-circuitry and *increases* the urge to continue (Clark et al., Neuron 2009).
- **The stopping cue is the whole game.** Infinite scroll removed the deliberation
  point where users quit; the involuntary auto-recompose does the same for live
  humans — stronger than Tinder's swipe (removes even the action to continue).
- **Anti-churn:** novelty is a decaying asset; Omegle/Houseparty died from *no
  memory* — no way to run into someone again, no investment layer.
- **Guardrails:** north-star must be *great moments per session*, not minutes;
  moderation/matching quality IS the retention engine; refuse fake presence, guilt
  streaks, asymmetric exit friction, compulsion-without-enjoyment (the "+37%
  compulsive checking without enjoyment" signature = a trap).

## B · The game engine — 30–90s video-native icebreakers

**Headline findings**
- Strangers on video default to **threat mode**; a game disarms it in ~10s via
  **attentional deflection** (a task, not a conversation prompt), **shared
  laughter** (oxytocin, mirror neurons), **reciprocal vulnerability** (everyone
  risks at once), and **"yes-and"** group-mind.
- **Laughter on live video is involuntary and contagious** — facial mimicry →
  emotional contagion; laughter has "transitive properties" that propagate with no
  shared context. One genuine crack infects the whole grid. This is the **moat**
  over text apps.
- **Three reusable loop skeletons:** Submit→Reveal→React (Jackbox/CAH),
  Simultaneous Action→Verdict (Most Likely To — fastest, most video-native),
  Pattern→Break (improv rhythm, try-not-to-laugh — zero content needed).
- **Peak-end rule:** engineer one structurally-funny detonation (a collision, a
  consensus roast, a visible crack) and **cut the game the instant the laugh
  lands.**
- **No losers, only champions;** spice lives in the *content*, never in humiliating
  a person; the shy player gets a guaranteed moment without performing.
- **Thin frame, fat deck:** each game = a template + a versioned prompt pool; the
  recomposing group is procedural content, so repeats never feel the same.

## C · The grab — first 3 seconds, the reveal, live presence

**Headline findings**
- **The window is <3s.** TikTok decides distribution at ~1.5s; ~65% who watch 3s
  reach 10s; 3s retention drives ~2.2× downstream engagement. Open **already
  alive** (motion before menus), never a static home.
- **Anticipation > reward.** The 1–2s before the stranger must be a **reveal
  countdown with a finish line**, not a spinner (a wait with a visible endpoint is
  ~10× more tolerable; a spinner reads as "broken"). Riser-that-resolves audio +
  haptic arc.
- **Live faces are neurochemically privileged:** eye contact/faces trigger oxytocin
  and co-presence; a stranger appearing from nowhere is the maximal **orienting
  response** (novel + social + real-time). **Pop, don't fade;** audio on at frame
  one.
- **Novelty habituates** — which is *why the skip exists*; each new face re-triggers
  the burst. Make NEXT a satisfying *throw*, the reload lever.
- **Two-phase self-view:** big/mirrored/auto-flattered *before* live (kills the "how
  do I look" fear — mere-exposure effect; always mirror); shrink to a corner PiP
  *once live* (self-monitoring is what causes camera anxiety / Zoom fatigue).
- **Liveness director:** PLAY must never return "nobody" — cascade exact→relaxed→
  wider-geo→small group→"warming up" countdown; concentrate supply into prime-time
  windows (density > headcount); warm pool; social proof at the door.
- **Onboarding:** ask camera/mic **at the moment of intent** (PLAY tap); run the 18+
  age check *inside* the flattering "getting ready" self-view; rig the first match.
